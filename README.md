## WHERE IS MY CAR! 🚗
##### *drag n drop car game made in unity*

Where is my car is a unity car game where the main goal is to drag all the cars into their correct places simmelar to a jigsaw puzzle

The game randomises the car positions every time the game is restarted so it's fun and replayable
the game screen is a bit zoomed in on your mouse for easier car spotting.

## TOWER OF HANOI 🗼
##### *classic puzzle game*

Tower of Hanoi is a classic puzzle game where you move a stack of discs from one peg to another. The goal is to stack all the discs on the **right peg** to win.
<br>
<img width="818" height="396" alt="Screenshot 2026-11-04 at 21 08 01" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/35a6438f-830d-4977-a295-d60c531b458d" />



#### *Choose your difficulty*
Before you start, you can choose how many discs you want to play with, from **3 to 6 discs**. More discs means a harder puzzle and more moves needed to finish.
<br>
<img width="451" height="87" alt="Screenshot 2026-11-04 at 21 06 11" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/f4804a1a-528a-465e-907e-7661a917faf1" />


#### *Star rating*
Your performance is rated with stars. The fewer moves you use to win, the more stars you earn. To get the highest star rating you have to solve the puzzle in as few moves as possible.
<br>
<img width="818" height="396" alt="Screenshot 2026-11-04 at 21 09 59" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/312f8424-337f-4e89-bb93-ea2abf5e515f" />


#### *Rewarded ad bonus*
Stuck on a high move count? You can watch a **rewarded ad** to remove **2 moves** from your total, which can help you reach a better star rating.
